# Travel Funding Guide for ML/AI Conferences

A personal reference for seeking travel grants, registration fee waivers, and volunteer opportunities at major ML/AI conferences (NeurIPS, CVPR, ICML, ICLR, and others). Contributions and updates are welcome via pull request.

> [!NOTE]
> **Purpose:** To help myself and other researchers, particularly those from low-resource institutions or regions, identify and access available funding mechanisms for conference participation.

## General Notes

- Industry travel grants are frequently paused or time-limited. Verify status on official portals before applying.
- Institute-level support varies significantly by department and supervisor. Contacting your supervisor or Dean (R&D) is recommended as a first step.
- Many conference aid programs require an accepted paper or proof of enrollment. Review eligibility criteria carefully before applying.
