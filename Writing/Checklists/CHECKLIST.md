# Checklist "before" requesting paper review

---

### Title / Subtitle

* Check capitalization in the title.
* Ensure consistent capitalization rules are applied to all section and subsection titles in the paper.
* If the Methods section is titled only “Method” or “Methodology,” revise it to a more specific title.

---

### References (\ref)

* Do not use references as nouns.

  (❌) “[5] proposed BLABLA” or “BLABLA was proposed in [5]”
  (🟢) Lim *et al.* [5] proposed BLABLA.

* In captions, use noun phrases (title-style). Also, do not use (a), (b), etc. as nouns within captions.

  (❌) (a) shows the ground truth map.
  (🟢) (a) Ground truth map.

---

### Using Ctrl+F in the PDF

* Verify that Tables and Figures are correctly referenced (common human error: mixing up Fig. # and Table #).
* Ensure there is no period after “Table” (the period is used for abbreviations like “Fig.”, not “Table”).
* Check proper capitalization of “Table” and “Fig.”
* Confirm correct formatting like “Fig. #(a)” (❌ “Fig. # (a)” with a space).
* Use `\Cref` where appropriate.

---

### Typos / Grammar

* Perform a final typo check using tools like Grammarly or ChatGPT.
* Errors in capitalization or subject-verb agreement often occur during revisions rather than initial drafting.
* Do not rely on a single grammar check—repeat checks after each revision to avoid mistakes like the ones below:

  * typos

---

### Equations

* Ensure `\begin{equation}` is written on the same line without a line break from the preceding text.
* Ensure `\end{equation}` is followed immediately by the sentence without a line break.
